### CHEM-000211: Material-based hydrogen storage meeting complete system targets
- **Subfield:** Porous coordination and covalent materials
- **Open:** Meet DOE ultimate usable system capacities of 6.5 wt% hydrogen and 50 g/L simultaneously with delivery, refueling, cost and lifetime requirements.
- **Status:** A decades-old target. Screening nearly half a million MOFs produced high-performing materials in 2019; complete systems must also account for vessels, cooling and unusable residual hydrogen.
- **Type:** moonshot
- **Why:** It would support compact hydrogen transport with competitive system mass and volume.
- **Key works:** U.S. DOE, *DOE Technical Targets for Onboard Hydrogen Storage for Light-Duty Vehicles*, [official table, accessed 2026-10-06](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/doe-technical-targets-onboard-hydrogen-storage-light-duty-vehicles); A. Ahmed et al. (2019), *Exceptional hydrogen storage achieved by screening nearly half a million metal-organic frameworks*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-019-09365-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-09365-w).
- **Related:** Energy engineering: rapid-fill thermal management. Optimization: simultaneous gravimetric and volumetric constraints.
